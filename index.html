About Mirrors Studio

We are Carolina & Alan, in Sydney. Mirrors Studio is where our drawings live: one-line flowers, celestial and coastal sets, little animals, and our graphic art.

We don't print or ship anything ourselves. Every design is sold through Redbubble and Zazzle, who print each order on demand and send it to you. Each design page links straight to it there.
